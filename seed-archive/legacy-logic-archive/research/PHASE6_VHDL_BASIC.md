# Phase 6: 1980s VHDL and BASIC magazine preservation

## Confirmed historical references
- IEEE 1076-1987 standard (approved Dec 10 1987, published Mar 31 1988): https://standards.ieee.org/ieee/1076/1609/ — bibliographic metadata only; standard text requires licensed access.
- VHDL-87 grammar transcription: https://iis-people.ee.ethz.ch/~zimmi/download/vhdl87_syntax.html — secondary transcription; date and redistribution rights unverified.
- The VHDL Handbook (1989) bibliographic discovery: https://www.scribd.com/document/463183982/1989-Bookmatter-TheVHDLHandbook — examples are described as 1076-1987 compliant, but no original source disk has been verified. Do not ingest full text without permission.
- Classic Computer Magazine Archive: https://www.atarimagazines.com/ — Antic, Compute!, Creative Computing, etc. Site permission to publish does NOT imply unrestricted bulk republication.
- Compute! issue index: https://www.atarimagazines.com/compute/ — 98 issues transcribed; beware OCR errors and missing source listings.
- TRS-80 magazine searchable index: https://www.trs-80.com/main-search-magazines.htm
- Creative Computing historical scans: https://www.trs-80.com/sub-mag-creative.htm
- Compute! scans: https://colorcomputerarchive.com/repo/Documents/Magazines/Compute/
- Example reconstructed type-in repository: https://github.com/bdowne01/compute — provenance and license pending.

## Classification and verification
VHDL: record exact revision (1987, 1993, unknown), entity/architecture, packages, simulator compatibility, source listing provenance, original creation year vs upload year.
BASIC: identify machine dialect (Commodore, Atari, AppleSoft, TRS-80, GW-BASIC, etc.), issue/date/page, original listing vs OCR transcription vs modern recreation; verify line numbers, checksums, DATA blocks and machine-code POKEs before execution.

## Rights and safety
Default to metadata-only for copyrighted magazine scans and standards. Public web accessibility is not a license to redistribute. Archive original bytes only after rights review; do not auto-execute magazine programs or untrusted hardware descriptions.
